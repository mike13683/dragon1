About Us

MOTORHOME & CAMPERVAN HIRE IN THE HEART OF THE EAST MIDLANDS

Welcome to Niche Motorhome Hire. We are a family owned business based near East Midlands Airport in the heart of the East Midlands. Whether you are looking for a Luxury Motorhome or cosy campervan to hire, you’ve come to the right place.

Having taken so much pleasure from our own camping trips, initially we decided to give others the joy of using our motorhome. As such all our vehicles come with everything you need to have a great trip no matter what the weather. Having hired motorhomes ourselves before buying our own we know what things were often missing so ours our loaded with things not usually found in hired Motorhomes. We also hated all those hidden extras so wanted to be transparent and provide a comprehensive motorhome with only a handful of truly optional extras.

Things have moved since we first started and we now have a range of luxury Motorhomes and Campervans to suit all tastes. Most people who hire Motorhomes or campervans are wanting to create their own experience. Are you ready to create yours? Want to take the family pet too? All our fleet are pet-friendly. Niche Motorhome Hire only has new and late model Motorhomes and VW campervans for hire. With cruise control and an automatic transmission, our fleet is easy to drive. Whilst all our fleet can be driven by people with a standard car licence.

Whether you are looking for a larger luxury motorhome or a more compact one we are certain to have one that will suit your needs. Motorhome & Campervan hire is the answer for touring the countryside in style. Touring holidays are economical, rewarding and fun. Most people hire motorhomes or campervan so they can view the picturesque landscape in comfort, our luxury fleet makes touring easy and fun. When you hire a motorhome or a campervan you are free to set your own rhythm, you can stop where you want and when you want. You are in control of your holiday with a motorhome.

That’s the beauty of choosing one of our luxury motorhomes to hire. Whether your trip is mapped out already, or you want to see where the wind takes you, you can be sure of a special and unforgettable getaway. There’s never been a better time to choose a staycation. Where will you go first? The tranquil Scottish highlands? The beautiful Lake District? The historic Yorkshire Dales? Or maybe a stunning sandy cove in Cornwall?

The great outdoors awaits you and your rental. Imagine driving along the open roads while the spectacular scenery unfolds before your eyes. Nothing beats sitting around a campfire, beneath the stars, knowing that a warm bed awaits you in the motorhome or campervan.

Whether you hire a motorhome or a campervan, you can pick it up directly from our base in the East Midlands. We have just introduced additional collection and delivery options to give you greater flexibility. Dropping off is easy and convenient too. Are you ready to create your own motorhome holiday or campervan adventure? Hire your easy to drive campervan or motorhome from Niche Motorhome Hire today.

Our Mission

We aim to offer our customers a great motorhome or campervan hire experience. With an easy and quick way to grab best deals on campervan / motorhome holidays. We allow you the freedom to hit the road, discover new places & create your own adventures and memories throughout the UK and Europe.

We believe in 100% Customer Satisfaction – We Live by It!

The easiest way to hire from us is, simply fill in the form on the booking page and then sit back & wait for us to confirm your requirements, alternatively you can continue to complete the booking yourself answering the required questions and entering your payment details.

If still unsure then please email or WhatApp us using the links at the top of the page instead. Once your booking deposit is received and confirmed your hire is secured we will then just need a copy of your driving licence and proof of address for the insurance a couple of weeks before you hire commences. You’re then set for your motorhome hire adventure to begin.
