Expedition - Premium Campervans

Perfect for couples or families wanting plenty of secure storage

Our Expedition Campervans are based upon the latest Fiat Ducato with Automatic gearbox.

The Auto Trail Expedition 68 with elevating roof can sleep up to 4 people.

The layout consists of a living area towards the front whilst the compact shower and toilet are opposite the kitchen area toward the middle, leaving the rear for an elevated bed can accommodate people up to 6ft with ease.

The permanently elevated bed provides for a huge garage. The garage can easily accommodate up to 4 bikes with front wheels removed and still have space for additional storage. The elevating roof with dedicated ladder provides the second double bed.

This award winning model is perfect those who want the freedom to roam but the security of having their bikes and equipment stored internally. There is a full kitchen, including 2 burner gas hob, gas oven, sink and fridge with freezer compartment.

Hot water to all taps and shower whilst a hot air distribution system provides central heating. Fly screens and blackout blinds are available for all windows and habitation door.

Reversing camera fitted to help manoeuvring, tracker also fitted. There are 4 travelling 3 point seat belts, including 2 Isofix seats in the forward facing rear seats.
